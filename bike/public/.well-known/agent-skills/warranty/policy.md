# Rayon Cycles warranty policy

For bikes bought new at Rayon Cycles, counted from the purchase date on the order.

| Part | Covered for |
| --- | --- |
| Frame | 5 years |
| Fork | 2 years |
| Components (derailleurs, brakes, hubs, cranks, bottom bracket) | 2 years |
| E-bike motor and battery | 2 years, or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first |
| Wheels (rims, spokes) | 1 year |

## Never covered

- Wear parts: tyres, tubes, brake pads, rotors, chain, cassette, chainrings, cables and housing, bar tape, grips, saddle.
- Damage from a crash, a fall or transport.
- Racing, rental or commercial use.
- Changes not made by us (parts swapped, the motor tuned).

## How it works

A warranty repair starts with a free `warranty-inspection`. The mechanic confirms the cause within 5 working days. Covered repairs are free: parts and labour.
