# ABC quick check

A two-minute check any rider can do before a ride. Walk them through it in order, one step at a time, and stop at the first thing that fails.

1. **Air.** Squeeze each tyre: it should barely give. The right pressure is printed on the tyre's side. Soft tyres pinch-flat and handle badly.
2. **Brakes.** Squeeze each lever: it must stop before touching the handlebar, and the wheel mustn't turn when you push the bike. A lever that sinks, or a squeal with weak braking, means **don't ride**: have the brakes checked.
3. **Chain.** Turn the pedals backwards: the chain should run quietly and look clean and lightly oiled. Rust, squeaks or jumping gears mean it needs care.
4. **Quick releases and thru-axles.** Each wheel's lever is closed and firm. A wheel that moves sideways is not safe.

Then drop the bike from a few centimetres: nothing should rattle.

If a step fails and the rider can't fix it on the spot (pump the tyre, close the lever), advise a check at a bike workshop before riding.
