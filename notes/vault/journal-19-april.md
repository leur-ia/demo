# Journal — 19 April

Unloaded the gas kiln today. Mixed feelings.

The good: the new [[Celadon]] batch came out exactly the way I've been chasing for a year, a cool grey-blue that goes deep in the carved lines. The body reduction held well this time; I kept the damper in for the full 40 minutes and it shows. Twelve bowls I'm genuinely happy with.

The bad: the whole top shelf of mugs. I'd dipped them in the new white matt, straight from the bucket, no test. On every single one the glaze had drawn up into fat little beads with bare clay showing between, like water on a waxed table. Too much raw kaolin in that recipe, I'm almost sure, and I put it on thick. Classic [[Crawling]]. Eighteen mugs for the shard pile. I know better. The [[Test tiles]] exist for exactly this.

And one of the big salad bowls split clean in two. I heard it go last night around 180 °C while I was locking up, a single ping. Glazed thickly inside, bare outside, and I opened the damper an inch too early because I wanted to get to bed. [[Dunting]], and entirely my fault.

Lessons for the wall:

1. Nothing new goes on a full load without tiles.
2. Damper stays shut until 150 °C.
3. Glaze both sides of wide bowls.

Twelve good celadon bowls still feel like a win. They go to [[The Saturday market]] next week.
