# Journal — 22 September

Back from the autumn [[Wood firing]], smelling of smoke and pine resin, with a burn on my left wrist and a box of pots I can't stop looking at.

Four days on the hill. I took the 2 a.m. to 6 a.m. stoking shift both nights, which I'd forgotten I love: the rhythm of feeding the firebox, the flame roaring out of the chimney, owls somewhere below. We reached cone 12 at the front on the third evening and held it for six hours.

The results:

- My unglazed jars on the second row have a thick honey-coloured coat on the shoulders, running in drips down one side. Best I've ever had.
- The [[Shino]] tea bowls went wild: heavy carbon trap, a blush of orange where the flame licked them.
- The small batch dipped in my stove [[Ash glaze]] looked almost identical to the natural deposit. Interesting. Maybe I can get close to this look in the gas kiln after all.

One lesson: two big [[Porcelain]] dishes came out with curling S-shaped lines across their bases. I rushed those floors and didn't compress them properly. [[S-cracks]], the oldest mistake. I'm going to spend the next throwing week drilling it back into my hands.

Now to price the good ones, photograph them, and choose three for the winter [[Tea mornings]].
