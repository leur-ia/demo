# Studio routine

My days only work if they have a shape. The clay has its own timetable (throwing, then waiting, then trimming, then waiting) and I've learned to organise myself around it.

A typical making day:

- **7:00** Kettle, open the windows, check what dried overnight.
- **7:30** [[Trimming]] whatever reached leather hard, while my hands are fresh.
- **9:00** [[Wedging]] and throwing a batch of 20–30 pots of one shape.
- **12:30** Lunch away from the bench, outside if possible.
- **13:30** Handles, lids, glazing, or kiln loading depending on the week.
- **16:00** Clean down: wet-mop the floor, sponge the benches, rinse the tools.
- **17:00** Write up the notebook: what I made, what's drying, what's going in the next firing.

The week has a rhythm too: making Monday to Wednesday, glazing and firing Thursday, and Friday for packing and admin before [[The Saturday market]].

The wet clean at the end isn't optional. It's the most important habit for [[Looking after hands and lungs]], and it's much easier to keep if it happens at the same time every day.

I don't always manage it. But on the days I do, I end tired in a good way, with the bench clean and tomorrow already half planned. Lately I've been guarding the first hour more jealously; [[Journal — 3 March]] is why.
