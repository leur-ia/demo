# Journal — 3 March

Woke at five and couldn't get back to sleep, so I walked down to the studio while it was still dark. First light was just coming grey over the hedge when I unlocked the door.

I didn't turn the radio on. Filled the kettle, listened to it tick and rumble, and stood with my hands around the mug while the window went from grey to pale gold. The only other sound was a blackbird on the roof of the kiln shed and the fridge humming.

There's a particular quiet in here before the lane wakes up. No van doors, no phone, no one waiting for an order. The clay on the bench was cold and a bit stiff from the night, and I spent a long time at the [[Wedging]] bench just because it felt good, the rocking rhythm of it.

Then I threw six [[Tea bowls]] without once looking at the clock. They're not perfect; one has a lip that dips more than I meant. But they have a looseness my afternoon pots never have. My hands were unhurried and the clay seemed to know it. [[Throwing on the wheel]] never feels like that when my head is already at the market.

By the time the neighbour's dog began barking it was nearly eight, and I'd done a day's worth of thinking. I want to protect this hour. Maybe rewrite the [[Studio routine]] so the first stretch belongs to nobody but me.
