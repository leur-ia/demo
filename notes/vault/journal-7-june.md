# Journal — 7 June

Best Saturday of the year so far. Sold 52 pots by half past one and drove home with an almost empty van.

The [[Tenmoku]] mugs were gone in the first hour, again. The pale blue bowls went steadily all day. The shelf of [[Tea bowls]] sat mostly untouched, as usual, until an older man spent twenty minutes turning each one over, looking at the feet, and finally bought the lopsided wood-fired one I nearly didn't bring.

A young couple asked whether I could make eight plates and eight bowls for their new house, all in the same glaze. That's the kind of thing I used to say yes to on the spot. This time I followed my own rules from [[Commissions]]: asked them to come to the studio next week, promised a fired sample, and said I'd send a written quote with a deposit. They didn't flinch at the numbers when I sketched them out, which tells me the new sums in [[Pricing my work]] are finally about right.

Takings, minus the stall fee and fuel for the van: best day since last September. More importantly, I talked to at least a dozen people who said they'd come to the next [[Tea mornings|tea morning]].

Note for next week: make twice as many tenmoku mugs, half as many small dishes. And bring a proper umbrella for the stall; the forecast looks grim for the rest of June.
