# Tea mornings

On the first Sunday of each month I open the studio for a tea morning. Six or eight people, sometimes regulars from [[The Saturday market]], sometimes neighbours who've walked past the open door for years.

It's simple:

1. Kettle on at nine, windows open, the bench cleared and wiped.
2. Everyone picks a cup or a bowl from the shelf of seconds and first tries.
3. I whisk a bowl of powdered green tea for anyone who wants one and brew a pot of roasted leaf tea for everyone else.
4. We talk, mostly about nothing, occasionally about pots.

I began it because pots are made to be used, and I wanted to watch strangers use mine. You learn more in an hour than in a year of making alone: which cups people reach for twice, which handles they ignore, whether the lid on the teapot rattles when it's passed around. After one of these I rethought the weight of my [[Tea bowls]] completely.

It also changed how I feel about selling. Nobody is asked to buy anything, but most months someone asks about a set, which has led to more than one of my [[Commissions]].

The teapot on the table is always the one from [[A teapot that pours well]], because it's the one I'm least embarrassed by.
